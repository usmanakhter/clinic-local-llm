Privacy Policy

Nepal Clinical Assistant · Android package np.clinical.clinical_assistant
Version aligned with in-app Terms np-terms-1.2
Last updated: 2026-08-05

This policy describes how the app handles information on your device and what may be sent to project servers after you accept the in-app Terms. It is not legal advice. Pilot / Play listing use may require counsel review.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to clinicians and other authorized users of the Nepal Clinical Assistant app (“the App”). The App is a clinical reference and drafting assistance tool — not a medical device for diagnosis, treatment decisions, or emergency care.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored locally:

  • Optional patient registry fields (e.g. name, age, phone, WhatsApp, email, notes)
  • Local history of searches, interaction checks, guideline lookups, chat turns, and note drafts
  • Terms acceptance / consent version records
  • Optional on-device language-model files you place on the device

Local history is kept in clinician-readable (unredacted) form for your use on that device. You are responsible for protecting the device and for complying with facility and professional privacy rules when entering patient information.

3. Information we sync (after Terms acceptance)

Accepting the in-app Terms (np-terms-1.2) is required to use the App. That acceptance turns scrubbed activity sync ON. There is no in-app switch to turn sync off later. If you do not want sync, do not accept the Terms and do not use the App.

May be synced (best-effort PII-scrubbed copies):

  • Drug searches, interaction checks, guideline lookups
  • Chat turns and clinical note drafts (scrubbed summaries)
  • Related metadata (e.g. query type, scrub timestamps, device id, consent version)

Not synced:

  • Local patient registry cards (names, phones, WhatsApp, etc. as stored on device)
  • Unredacted on-device clinical history
  • Payloads that fail the residual-PII gate (they remain on device)

Production ingest is intended for project servers in the India region (Supabase Mumbai / ap-south-1) when configured in a release build.

4. Purposes of sync

Synced scrubbed activity may be used to:

  • Improve the clinical knowledge base / retrieval corpus
  • Improve model and product quality

We do not sell identifiable clinical content to life insurers or employers, and we do not use data for automated diagnosis of individuals.

5. PII scrubbing (best effort)

Before upload, the App attempts to scrub personally identifiable information (such as phone numbers, emails, and certain structural IDs) from sync payloads. Scrubbing is best effort and does not guarantee removal of all identifiers (for example, free-text names or place names may remain). Residual re-identification risk can remain after scrubbing.

6. Third parties and analytics

Sync uses cloud infrastructure operated for this project (currently planned via Supabase in Mumbai when enabled). The App is not built on Firebase Analytics for clinical session egress. Device OS services (e.g. Google Play services for distribution/updates) may process standard install/update data under their own policies.

7. Retention and your choices

  • On-device data remains until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Stopping use of the App is the way to stop future sync of new activity.
  • Material Terms changes require renewed in-app acceptance.

8. Children

The App is intended for adult clinicians and authorized health workers, not for children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material product changes to data practices are also reflected in updated in-app Terms that require re-acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: open an issue on the project repository usmanakhter/clinic-local-llm.
